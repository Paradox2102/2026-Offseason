# Sub-project 2: intake, implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A rack that zeroes at boot, measures its out stop on the first extend, keeps both sides
in step, and survives a Talon reboot; rollers with jam recovery; all tested in sim.

**Architecture:** Two subsystems (`IntakeRack`, `IntakeRoller`) with real Talons, pure helper
classes (`StallDetector`, `SkewGuard`) with JUnit tests, sim physics in `IntakeSim`.

**Tech Stack:** as sub-project 1.

**Changed while building (user, 2026-10-22):** every move ends with a push into its stop and
re-trues there, per side; the rack rests on a light 2 A push and does not fight hits; the skew
guard only alerts. Task 2 tests follow `RackSideTest` as built. See the spec section "Hits,
skipped teeth, rest".

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-22-nick-test-rewrite-design.md`, section "Sub-project 2: intake".

## Global Constraints

- Global constraints of `2026-10-22-sp1-foundation-drive.md` apply.
- Rack units: meters (0 = in, 0.324 = out). Roller units: roller rps.
- All Talon configs go through `PhoenixUtil.applyConfig`.
- Alerts through `AlertPublisher.create("Health", ...)` with ids under `intake/`.

## Review Focus

1. **Talon 22 reboots while extended:** it reads 0 at 0.3 m out. That side is "lost": the skew
   guard must ignore it, and its next move pushes into a stop and resets it. Test in Task 2
   (state logic with `onReset()`).
2. **First extend never reaches the out stop (blocked by a game piece or a wall):** timeout,
   alert, use the CAD stroke, no crash. Test in Task 2.
3. **Retract interrupted by extend (driver changes mind) during the first out-stop push:** the
   push restarts cleanly; nothing is measured from a half push.
4. **Trigger released mid-extend:** the rack continues to the goal (stays out), rollers stop.
5. **Robot booted with the rack out (someone forgot):** 0 is then wrong, and the first push finds
   the out stop at a small position. A measured stop outside 85 % to 115 % of the CAD stroke means
   a bad boot zero: set that side's position to the CAD stroke at the stop, and alert "Intake was
   not in at boot". Test `implausibleStopFixesZero` in Task 2.
