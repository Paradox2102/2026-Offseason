# Sub-project 5: fuel physics and the bump in the sim

Spec: docs/specs/2026-10-22-nick-test-rewrite-design.md, "Sub-project 5". Native execution.

1. `sim/field/BumpTerrain` (pure, tested): `height(x, y)`, `slopeX(x, y)`, `pose3d(Pose2d)`,
   `groundFactor(Pose2d)`. Tests: flat floor 0; peak 0.165 at the bump center; 15 degree slope
   on a ramp; a robot with its front wheels on the ramp pitches nose up (negative pitch); the red
   bumps mirror the blue ones; factor 1 off the bump, cos(15)x0.9 on a ramp.
2. `sim/field/FuelField` (pure, tested): balls, hopper, `step(dt, RobotInput)`, `launch(...)`,
   counts. Tests: a ball rolls and stops; a ball rolls down a ramp; the robot pushes a ball out of
   its bumpers; the running intake picks a ball up, the hopper stops at 40; a raised intake or
   stopped rollers push instead; a ball dropped into the hub opening scores and comes back out on
   the neutral side; a ball that misses lands on the floor; balls never leave the field.
3. `sim/FuelSim` (wiring): reads the drive truth, intake, rollers, feeder, flywheel, hood and
   turret; shoots at 8/s while feeding; publishes `Sim/Fuel`, `RobotPose3d` and the counts; loads
   `deploy/sim/fuel-start.csv`; reset at auto start and with "Sim/Reset Fuel".
4. `DriveSim`: truth integrates the wheel travel times `groundFactor`; `truthPose3d()`. Vision
   sim updates from the 3D truth.
5. Smoke test lines and an Elastic widget pair (hopper, scored) on the Tuning tab.
6. `./gradlew build`, `tools/sim-smoke.sh` blue and red, commit, push to nick-test.
